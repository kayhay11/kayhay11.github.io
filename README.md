## *Personal Assessment*
Throughout my computer science program, I have grown from a beginner learning foundational concepts into a confident developer capable of designing, analyzing, and improving real systems. Completing the coursework and building my ePortfolio has helped me articulate the values that shape the kind of engineer I want to be. The process of selecting and reflecting on my artifacts has allowed me to demonstrate not only what I can build  but how I think and solve problems. 
One of the most important areas of growth for me has been learning to collaborate effectively in team environments. Across multiple courses I worked on shared codebases and participated in peer reviews. These experiences taught me how to communicate clearly and integrate feedback constructively. I learned that collaboration is not just about writing code together but ensuring that everyone understands the system’s design and goals. These skills translate directly to professional software development where teamwork and communication are essential.

Communicating with stakeholders has also been a recurring theme in my coursework. Whether writing technical documentation, presenting project updates, or explaining design decisions, I learned how to tailor my communication to different audiences. In several assignments I had to justify architectural choices or translate technical concepts into accessible language. These experiences strengthened my ability to bridge the gap between technical implementation and user or stakeholder needs. 
My coursework in data structures and algorithms provided the foundation for writing efficient, scalable code. I gained hands-on experience implementing linked lists, trees, sorting algorithms, and hash-based structures, and learned how algorithmic complexity affects performance. These concepts appear throughout my work, even in projects that do not explicitly focus on algorithms. Understanding how data flows through a system and how operations scale has shaped the way I approach problem-solving and system design. Software engineering and database courses helped me develop a disciplined approach to building applications. I learned how to design modular systems, apply object-oriented principles, and use version control effectively. Working with SQL and relational database design taught me how to structure data and write queries that support application logic. These skills strengthened my ability to build complete, maintainable systems rather than isolated pieces of code. 

Security has been another important area of growth. Through coursework and hands-on projects, I learned how vulnerabilities arise and why secure coding practices matter. This allowed me to gain experience analyzing authentication logic and understanding how attackers exploit weaknesses. This perspective has influenced how I write and review cod, ensuring that I consider not only functionality but also safety and ethical responsibility.
These experiences shaped the artifacts included in my ePortfolio. Each artifact highlights a different aspect of my abilities including embedded systems programming, debugging, interface design, secure coding, and system enhancement. The thermostat project, for example, demonstrates my ability to work with hardware inputs, manage state transitions, and refine user interaction logic. Other artifacts showcase my skills in software engineering, algorithmic thinking, and secure development practices. Collectively, they form a cohesive representation of my capabilities as a developer and illustrate how my coursework has prepared me for professional work.

Building this ePortfolio has helped me recognize how far I have come and where I want to go. I value clarity, reliability, and user-centered design, and I aim to continue developing as a full-stack or embedded systems engineer. The combination of technical knowledge, collaborative experience, and security awareness I gained throughout the program has made me more employable and better prepared to contribute meaningfully to the computer science field. This self-assessment serves as an introduction to the technical artifacts that follow, each one demonstrating a different dimension of my growth and the skills I will bring into my professional career.
